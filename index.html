MemoSeed（回想）隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

MemoSeed（回想）由 Zerocode Studio 提供。如有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 zero.core.code@gmail.com。应用内的「设置 → 隐私政策」也提供离线版本。

设备内保存的信息

日常、随记、标签、提醒和应用设置保存在设备的应用存储中。应用不要求注册账号，也不会把你的文字上传到 Zerocode Studio 的服务器。你可以使用密码及系统生物识别限制进入应用；应用没有宣称本地数据库已加密。

在线句子服务

刷新句子时，应用通过 HTTPS 向 v1.hitokoto.cn 请求内容。请求包含句子分类，不包含日常或随记正文。该服务可能按自己的规则获取 IP 地址等网络信息。应用没有集成广告或统计分析 SDK。

可选的导出与备份

只有选择导出或备份后，应用才会写入你指定的本地位置、SMB 共享或 WebDAV 服务器。备份 ZIP 可包含日常、随记、标签、提醒、设备名称和应用版本。备份 ZIP 本身未加密，请保护存放位置并使用可信的服务器；传输安全取决于所选地址和连接。远程备份凭据在应用本地使用 Android Keystore 加密保存。

设备权限

通知、精确闹钟及开机广播用于提醒；生物识别用于可选的应用锁；网络用于在线句子和可选的远程备份。Android 9 可能使用存储权限导出到下载目录。这些权限不会让 Zerocode Studio 读取你的记录。

保留与删除

本地内容会保留到你在应用中删除它，或清除应用数据为止。应用外的导出与备份不会因卸载应用而自动删除，需要在相应位置自行删除。应用没有需要注销的账号。Zerocode Studio 不托管你的记录副本。

联系

如有隐私问题，请联系 Zerocode Studio：zero.core.code@gmail.com。应用处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策。

MemoSeed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

MemoSeed (回想) is provided by Zerocode Studio. For privacy questions, contact zero.core.code@gmail.com. An offline copy is available in Settings → Privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Daily entries, notes, tags, reminders and app settings are stored in the app’s local storage. The app does not require an account and does not upload your writing to a Zerocode Studio server. A PIN and system biometric authentication can restrict access to the app; the local database itself is not advertised as encrypted.

Online quote service

When the quote feature refreshes, the app requests a quote from v1.hitokoto.cn over HTTPS. The request includes quote categories, not your entries or notes. The service can see network information such as your IP address under its own practices. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Optional exports and backups

If you choose to export or back up data, the app writes to the local location, SMB share or WebDAV server you select. A backup ZIP can contain your entries, notes, tags, reminders, device name and app version. Backup ZIP files are not encrypted; protect the destination and use a trusted server. Transfer security depends on your chosen destination and connection. Remote-backup credentials are encrypted in the app’s local storage using Android Keystore.

Device permissions

Notification, exact-alarm and boot-completed access support reminders. Biometric access supports the optional app lock. Network access supports quotes and optional remote backups. On Android 9, storage permission can be used to export to Downloads. These permissions do not grant Zerocode Studio access to your entries.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it in the app or remove the app’s data. Exports and backups outside the app are not removed by uninstalling it; delete them at their destination. There is no app account to delete. Zerocode Studio does not host a copy of your entries.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Zerocode Studio at zero.core.code@gmail.com. We may update this policy when app behavior changes.
